# ActiveForcing table-push V2: A2.1 falsified

V1 remains untouched and valid. Its all-four-branch forensic audit confirms the early frozen lateral direction differs from later nominal forward motion, motivating—but not validating—the V2 causal fresh-chunk estimator.

V2's estimator passed synthetic tests and offline checks on the successful audit action trace plus all four V1 traces. In A2.1, every matched root-0/mu-1.2 grid episode had exact friction readback, retained contact, finite telemetry, causal direction persistence, XY-only residuals, and independently reconstructed raw-contact sums/projections.

Nevertheless, the preregistered steady aligned-force means across normalized residuals `[-.12, -.06, 0, +.06, +.12]` were `[39.621, 43.068, 45.765, 45.367, 35.683] N`. The response is not monotonic (and has Pearson correlation −0.2076); the largest positive intervention collapses and is high-variance. There is no observed overlapping controllable range from which to freeze Newton targets. This falsifies A2.1.

Accordingly A2.2, task-recovery trials, Gate B/C/D, and the 216-branch collection were not started.
