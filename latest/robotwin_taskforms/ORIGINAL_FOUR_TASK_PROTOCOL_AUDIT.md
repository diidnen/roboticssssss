The paper's 96 main contexts are a later, separate online-VLA evaluation, not the 96-row physical-belief prediction table and not the earlier 72-context E1 archive.

All 384 branches record real online pi0 inference using checkpoint hash `0598a390733235fde0bf5633b1543d91176d31bb5012d4d26f9373a90642fe17`. The policy was stochastic. Its noise seed was deterministically derived from `(context_id, replan_step)` and matched across method siblings on their shared online-query prefix. Across the pooled evaluation there are 96 unique initial online-chunk hashes, one per physical context, with zero within-context mismatches across the four methods. Subsequent chunks were generated online from each branch's current observations; they were not replayed trajectories.

The main roots are disjoint from feasibility roots 5100, 5101, 5102, 5106, 6100, and 6103. Main root selection was prospective and outcome-blind. The second four roots were explicitly verified untouched before execution.

## Data-quality conclusion

The final protocol is sufficiently unambiguous to define the new-task experiment. The key comparability requirements are root-family separation, all three friction strata in each split, 4/1/1 feasibility roots, entirely fresh main-evaluation roots, and within-context method pairing. A held-out policy-seed split is not part of the original protocol and must not be made a mandatory success condition for the new-task main experiment.
